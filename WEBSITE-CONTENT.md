### A chart you can check.

Every placement is calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris in the Lahiri sidereal system. That covers planets, houses, nakshatras, divisional charts (D7, D9, D10) and the dasha tree. A language model does not invent a planetary position.

That rule matters because astrology tools are easy to fake. A fluent paragraph proves nothing. A chart that matches the one an astrologer would cast by hand gives every reading something concrete to stand on.

### The reading names its periods.

From the chart, Trikaal Darshi writes eleven chapters: lagna, Lal Kitab, numerology, career, wealth, marriage, health, remedies, progeny, current transits and education. Each prediction is placed inside a named dasha window, so a career change arrives with a period instead of a platitude.

Readers can ask follow-up questions against their own chart and the chapters already written for it. It is not a general astrology chatbot answering from the internet.

### What it does.

| Feature           | Description                                                                                           |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Calculated charts | A sidereal Lahiri chart with planets, houses, nakshatras, divisional charts and the dasha tree.       |
| Eleven chapters   | A full reading across career, wealth, marriage, health, remedies, transits and more.                  |
| Ask the chart     | Follow-up questions answered from that person's chart and its written chapters.                       |
| Working tools     | Daily panchang, personal transits, 36-guna compatibility, birth-time rectification and a PDF dossier. |

### Who it's for.

Serious readers of Vedic astrology who want their own chart explained in their own language, and practitioners who need a correct first-pass dossier in minutes instead of an afternoon.

### Good to know.

- Positions are calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris in the Lahiri sidereal system. Readings are written from that chart; planetary positions are not generated by a language model.
- Reading passes are paid by UPI on trikaldarshi.online. Payments, access and any related support are handled there.
- The complete reading can be downloaded as a PDF dossier.

## AmyLearn — `amylearn.html` (`/amylearn`)

**Meta title:** AmyLearn — Academic platform for colleges | Amyreil
**Meta description:** AmyLearn is a multi-tenant academic platform for attendance, examinations, learning, library and fee records. In beta, with a live demo on request.

### Hero

**Status:** Beta
**Headline:** AmyLearn
**Lead:** A multi-tenant academic platform that runs a college's attendance, examinations, learning, library and fee records from one role-based workspace.
**Buttons:** Request a live demo · Talk to the studio

**Facts**

| Label   | Value                               |
| ------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Status  | Beta                                |
| Type    | ERP + LMS                           |
| Portals | Students, faculty, exam cell, staff |
| AI      | Study desk                          |

**Mockup labels:** amylearn / semester-4 · Semester 4 / CSE Odd semester · Week 6 · 94% Attendance · 12 Open assignments · 4 Classes today · Data Structures · 62 students 10:30–11:20

### Every institution keeps its own space.

Each college joins with its own college code. Every record is tenant-scoped, so one institution never sees another's students, marks or fees.

Students, faculty, exam cells and platform staff use separate portals. Roles, menus and feature flags are configured per institution, with email-verified signup, JWT sessions, bulk import and audit logs.

### The week runs from one workspace.

Departments, streams, subjects, batches and classrooms are structured data, not spreadsheets. Faculty take attendance, submit weekly reports, set assignments, grade submissions and post notices inside the same tenant.

A shared calendar and comments keep the handoffs in one place instead of across three departments.

### Exams move as one lifecycle.

Exam sessions, eligibility, forms, seat allocation, question bank, marks upload and result publishing sit in one flow. Admit cards carry QR or barcode checks, and semester feedback follows the result.

The exam cell stops rebuilding the same student list for every stage.

### A study desk and a library.

The AI study desk is a Socratic tutor with mind maps, document summaries and OCR for PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX and image files, plus breakdowns of public YouTube lectures.

The library handles digital and physical checkout, returns and overdue fines in the same workspace.

### Who it's for.

Small and mid-sized colleges, university departments and multi-campus groups. The people using it are the exam cell, academic office, faculty and students. The buyer is usually academic or IT leadership replacing a patchwork of systems.

### Good to know.

- The fee module records a ledger and receipts. No payment gateway is connected, so online fee payment is not part of the product.
- The AI study desk is a study assistant, not an assessor. It is not a grading tool.
- Assignment submissions store file references rather than uploaded files.
- Inference runs on Groq-hosted open-source models. Built with React 18, Vite, Express, Prisma, JWT authentication and Tesseract OCR, deployed on Vercel and Render.

## HotelEase — `hotelease.html` (`/hotelease`)

**Meta title:** HotelEase — Offline hotel management for Windows | Amyreil
**Meta description:** HotelEase v2.2 is a Windows front-desk app for small hotels: room board, check-in, GST invoices and encrypted local data. Works without internet.

### Hero

**Status:** Available · v2.2
**Headline:** HotelEase
**Lead:** A Windows front-desk app for small hotels that handles rooms, check-ins and GST invoices on one PC, with or without internet.
**Button:** Ask about HotelEase

**Facts**

| Label    | Value         |
| -------- | ------------- |
| Version  | 2.2           |
| Platform | Windows       |
| Mode     | Offline-first |
| Billing  | GST invoices  |

**Mockup labels:** hotelease v2.2 / front-desk · Front desk 12 rooms · Tue 28 · 4 arrivals · 2 departures · 4 invoices · room states: Ready, In · 4 pm, Free, In · 2 pm

### Built for a desk that cannot stop.

Most small hotels still run the desk on a paper register, a spreadsheet and a handwritten bill. HotelEase puts those jobs in one Windows app: a live room board, guest records with ID proof, and a GST invoice generated from the same booking.

There is no cloud login and no reconnecting screen. A dropped line does not stop check-ins or billing.

### The data stays in the building.

Records are stored in an encrypted file on the reception PC. SQLCipher AES-256 encrypts the database, Windows DPAPI protects the key, and a backup runs every time the app opens. The last five backups are kept, and a full encrypted export can be written to an external drive.

### What it does.

| Feature               | Description                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Offline front desk    | Live room board, check-in and check-out, guest profiles with ID proof and past stays, on one PC.                                                    |
| GST invoices          | CGST and SGST split at your configured rate, sequential invoice numbers, a PDF generated from the booking and reprintable from the built-in viewer. |
| Encrypted local data  | SQLCipher AES-256, Windows DPAPI key protection, automatic backups on launch and an encrypted export to external storage.                           |
| Audit trail and roles | Logins, check-ins, check-outs and edits record the user, machine, time and before/after values. Admin, Manager and Receptionist roles limit access. |

### Who it's for.

Owner-run hotels, lodges and guest houses with roughly 10 to 50 rooms, one reception PC and a small front-desk team. They have no IT staff, often run on paper or Excel, and cannot absorb downtime or billing mistakes.

The first market is Bardhaman and Durgapur in West Bengal, then Tier 2 and Tier 3 India.

### Good to know.

- v2.2 is a single-PC Windows app. Hotel records stay in an encrypted local file; there is no cloud sync.
- First install may need internet if Microsoft Edge WebView2 is not already on the PC.
- GST invoices use the rate you configure. HotelEase generates and reprints bills; it does not file returns.
