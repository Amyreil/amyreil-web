No clones. Only products with a reason to exist.

We learn the real problem, the people affected and what already exists. The step ends with a one-page problem statement, not a feature list.

Problem statementUsersConstraints

Define

We agree scope, timeline, price and what done means, and write it down. Work starts only after that scope is approved.
