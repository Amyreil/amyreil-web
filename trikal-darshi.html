A chart you can check.

Every placement is calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris in the Lahiri sidereal system. That covers planets, houses, nakshatras, divisional charts (D7, D9, D10) and the dasha tree. A language model does not invent a planetary position.

That rule matters because astrology tools are easy to fake. A fluent paragraph proves nothing. A chart that matches the one an astrologer would cast by hand gives every reading something concrete to stand on.

The reading names its periods.

From the chart, Trikaal Darshi writes eleven chapters: lagna, Lal Kitab, numerology, career, wealth, marriage, health, remedies, progeny, current transits and education. Each prediction is placed inside a named dasha window, so a career change arrives with a period instead of a platitude.

Readers can ask follow-up questions against their own chart and the chapters already written for it. It is not a general astrology chatbot answering from the internet.

What it does.

Calculated charts

A sidereal Lahiri chart with planets, houses, nakshatras, divisional charts and the dasha tree.

Eleven chapters

A full reading across career, wealth, marriage, health, remedies, transits and more.

Ask the chart

Follow-up questions answered from that person's chart and its written chapters.

Working tools

Daily panchang, personal transits, 36-guna compatibility, birth-time rectification and a PDF dossier.

Who it's for.

Serious readers of Vedic astrology who want their own chart explained in their own language, and practitioners who need a correct first-pass dossier in minutes instead of an afternoon.

Good to know.

  • Positions are calculated with the Swiss Ephemeris in the Lahiri sidereal system. Readings are written from that chart; planetary positions are not generated by a language model.
  • Reading passes are paid by UPI on trikaldarshi.online. Payments, access and any related support are handled there.
  • The complete reading can be downloaded as a PDF dossier.
