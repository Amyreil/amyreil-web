Why we exist.

Amyreil began with a simple belief: great technology starts with asking the right questions. Instead of copying what already exists, we look past the familiar approach to find a better way to solve the problem.

We build original SaaS products and deliver technology services to businesses and individuals. Our clients are usually small and mid-sized teams that want a practical solution without the overhead of a large agency.

We do not build a product unless we can explain in one sentence why it should exist and who it helps. That sentence comes before any code.

Where we're going.

VisionTo become a leading source of original SaaS innovation, building for the challenges others overlook.
MissionTo build practical, original software and AI solutions that remove unnecessary steps from everyday work.

What we hold to.

EfficiencyWe question every feature and process: does this remove effort, or create it?
IntegrityWe admit mistakes early, keep our deadlines and never overstate what a product can do.
EffectivenessWe test with real users and real data before calling something done.
InnovationWe research what already exists, then propose something better instead of a clone.
Purposeful productsEvery project starts with a written problem statement and a clear user.

How we work with clients.

One point of contactEvery project has a named lead who owns communication, so you are not routed through an account team.
Written scopeWork starts after scope and price are agreed in writing. Changes are agreed the same way.
Honest updatesA written progress note every week, and risks raised when we see them, not at the deadline.
No overpromisingWe say what we can deliver and when, and we say no when something does not fit.
